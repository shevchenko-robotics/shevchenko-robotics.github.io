About

I have worked in robotics for more than eleven years. I started at RSC Energia doing R&D in space robotics, then moved into universities.

Today I teach at Bauman Moscow State Technical University and Moscow State University of Technology “STANKIN”, supervise student projects, and teach an English-language course on industrial robots abroad. I also write professional training programs for engineers.

What interests me is where a robot meets a real task: an arm on the shop floor or a mobile platform that finds its own way. In teaching, what matters most to me is that my students can work with real equipment as soon as they graduate.

For students

I supervise student theses. These are the topics I am most interested in right now:

  • computer vision and neural networks for tracking moving objects;
  • control systems for pan-tilt and tracking devices;
  • industrial and collaborative robots: programming, robotic cells, simulation;
  • mobile robots on ROS 2: navigation, localization, simulation;
  • space robotics.

I meet each thesis student once a week. Students keep all their work in a GitHub repository based on a template; finished projects are collected in vkr-robotics.

How do I choose a topic?

Pick one of the topics above or propose your own. We refine it at the first meeting so that it interests you and fits the time you have.

What should I prepare for the first meeting?

A few words about yourself: what you have built, which languages and tools you know, what you would like to try. If you have a GitHub account, send me the link.

How does the work go?

We meet once a week, review what is done and set tasks for the next week. Code, calculations, drafts and notes live in a repository based on the template, so we both see the progress.

If you would like to do your thesis with me, message me on Telegram: who you are, where you study, and what topic interests you. You can also propose your own.
